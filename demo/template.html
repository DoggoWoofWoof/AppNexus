  1. The sample day has appointments at 10:00 and 14:00. Open Manage visit and choose Doctor cancelled.
  2. Inspect an alternative, use the slot for a visit or call, or leave it open. The other appointment stays fixed.
  3. Open the map from Today or switch between Map and List in Targets.
  4. Open Prepare visit, add a report/insight inline, or manage the appointment. Request a mock office response directly, or keep an unsent draft.
  5. Review or edit the conversation in Activity and complete the linked follow-up.
  6. Log a visit and follow-up, then explore the short HCP brief, full profile sections and Insights tools.

Plans, notes and received audio chunks are stored on this browser and survive refresh. Sample recordings are tones; their transcripts are fixtures. Transcription, reports, office responses, availability and driving times are mocked. No production API, CRM or cloud backup is connected. Map geography is real; people and activity are illustrative.
